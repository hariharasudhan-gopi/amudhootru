# 4. Return & Refund Policy

Because we sell food and consumable products, returns are subject to product condition and applicable requirements.

### Damaged Products

If a product is received damaged, customers should contact us as soon as possible after delivery.

Please provide:

* Order number
* Description of the issue
* Photographs of the product and packaging

Our team will review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

### Wrong Product Delivered

If you receive a product different from the one ordered, please contact us immediately with your order details.

We will review the issue and assist with an appropriate resolution.

### Product Quality Issues

If you believe that a product has a quality issue, please contact us promptly after receiving the order.

The product may be reviewed based on the details provided, including the product condition and packaging.

For hygiene, safety, and quality reasons, opened or used food products may generally not be eligible for return.

* Honey

Any exception will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

### Refunds

If a refund is approved, the refund will be processed through the applicable payment method or another appropriate method communicated by Amudhootru Organics.

The time taken for the refund to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider or banking institution.
