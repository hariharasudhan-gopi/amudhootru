## Groundnut Oil

### 4. What is groundnut oil?

Groundnut oil is a natural edible oil extracted from groundnuts (peanuts). It is commonly used for cooking, frying, and other food preparations.

### 5. How should I store groundnut oil?

Store the oil in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep the container tightly closed when not in use.

### 6. Can groundnut oil be used for cooking?

Yes. Groundnut oil is commonly used for everyday cooking, frying, and preparing various traditional dishes.

---

## Groundnuts

### 7. What are groundnuts?

Groundnuts, also known as peanuts, are nutrient-rich edible seeds commonly consumed as a snack or used in cooking.

### 8. How should I store groundnuts?

Store groundnuts in a cool, dry place in an airtight container. Keeping them away from moisture helps maintain their freshness and quality.

---

## Coconut Oil

### 9. What is coconut oil used for?

Coconut oil can be used for cooking and food preparation. Depending on the product and customer preference, coconut oil may also be used for traditional household and personal-care purposes.

### 10. How should I store coconut oil?

Store coconut oil in a cool, dry place and keep the container tightly closed. Coconut oil may naturally become solid or liquid depending on the surrounding temperature. This is normal.

---

## Honey

### 11. Is honey a natural product?

Yes. Honey is a naturally produced food made by bees. We focus on providing quality honey while maintaining its natural characteristics.

### 12. Why does honey sometimes become thick or crystallize?

Natural honey can crystallize or become thicker over time. This is a natural process and does not necessarily mean that the honey has spoiled.

### 13. How should I store honey?

Store honey at room temperature in a cool, dry place. Keep the container tightly closed and use a clean, dry spoon when serving.
